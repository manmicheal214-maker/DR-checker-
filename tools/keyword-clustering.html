How this content map generator works

Keywords are normalized conservatively, compared by exact core-token overlap, and given a small bonus for a deliberately limited set of synonym groups. Candidate pairs are generated efficiently before the selected similarity threshold is applied. Primary keywords use lexical centrality rather than search volume.

Modifier tags are pattern-based signals, not confirmed search-intent classifications. Cohesion is an average pairwise similarity statistic, not a confidence or accuracy score.

Review borderline cases

Lexical clustering cannot detect true synonyms it does not know about, fully resolve short phrases that touch two topics at once, or know search volume unless you have that data elsewhere. A phrase such as “seo audit tool” can legitimately sit between concepts. Use the remove and primary-keyword controls to review and adjust borderline cases.

Frequently asked questions

Does this upload my keywords?

All processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Does a cluster mean the keywords have the same search intent?

No. Modifier tags are only pattern-based signals, and lexical similarity is not a validated intent classifier.

Can I treat the clusters as final?

No. Review and adjust borderline cases. The tool has no search-volume data and deliberately uses conservative lexical and synonym rules.
